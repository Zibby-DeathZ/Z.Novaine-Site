Ink and Ember

Somewhere between the dusk and the dark
a name was born from blight and thorn,
half a whisper, half a spark,
neither night nor morn.

I write the wound before the kiss,
the crown before the fall,
the joke that hides what love is,
the silence after all.

Every story starts as smoke,
a shape without a name,
until the pen decides to choke
the dark into a flame.

So call me shadow, call me light,
call me neither, call me bright,
I am only what I write,
and to write,I happily oblige.

~Z.Novaine
