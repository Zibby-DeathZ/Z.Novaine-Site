Vol. I - Dark Fantasy

Forsaken: To Bleed for Her

Varek Feronis was born to rule a kingdom that fears what runs in his blood. Cursed with a magic that marks him for death in a world that hunts magic users, the crown prince of Virelya has one choice: bleed quietly, or burn everything down. A story of blood, crowns, and the price of power, for readers who like their romance dangerous and their kingdoms crumbling.
