About

Z. Novaine writes stories that live in the space between shadow and light. Whether crafting the blood soaked tragedy of a cursed prince or the sharp witted chaos of a slow burn romance, Novaine's work is united by one obsession: characters who feel real enough to bleed, laugh, and love on the page.

Novaine's writing draws from years of storytelling across mediums, blending the emotional intensity of epic fantasy with the comedic timing of modern romance. Readers can expect richly built worlds, morally complex heroes, and a narrative voice that refuses to hold back.

Alongside the novels, Novaine publishes a new short story or poem every month under Fragments, small, standalone pieces free for anyone to read.

When not writing, Novaine can usually be found deep in a video game, sketching character concepts, or getting lost in the kind of stories that started this obsession in the first place.

This is only the beginning.
